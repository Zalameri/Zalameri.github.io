AquaGent

An explainable decision-support agent that chooses between a fog collector, a MOF-303 sorbent bed, or no plan, when weather evidence is uncertain and energy is limited.

Zainab Alameri & Abubaker Baobaid · Instructor: Dr. Naila Marir

01 · OverviewWhy this problem

Water from air can help dry regions such as Saudi Arabia, but there are two very different ways to collect it. A fog mesh catches droplets that are already in the air, so it needs fog and wind. A MOF-303 bed adsorbs water vapour and needs energy to release it. Humidity alone cannot tell an operator which one to run.

How can an agent choose a fog collector, a MOF bed, or no safe option when weather evidence is uncertain and resources are limited?
Adversarial challenge (course theme)

Noisy observations: the fog sensor can report fog that is not there, or miss real fog.

Incomplete knowledge: the fog reading or the humidity value can be missing or invalid.

Probabilistic reasoning

A Bayesian update turns an unreliable sensor into a probability of fog.

Constraint satisfaction

A backtracking CSP removes plans that break safety, time, energy or weather limits.

1 · Bayesian sensor model

P(F | E+) = s · πs · π + (1 − c)(1 − π)

Defaults π = 0.35, s = c = 0.90 (assumptions). A missing reading keeps the prior and raises a warning.

2 · Backtracking CSP over method × hours

  • Safety check passed
  • H ≤ maximum hours
  • H × rate ≤ energy budget
  • Fog: P(F|E) ≥ 0.75
  • Fog: wind > 0, LWC > 0
  • MOF: RH > 0

3 · Estimate and rank

Lfog = 3.6 · LWC · v · A · η · H · P(F | E)
LMOF = 0.210 · mMOF · H / 24   ·   S = L1 + E / Ebudget

Energy rates 0.10 and 1.20 kWh/h are demonstration values. The MOF rate comes from Song et al. (2023).

05 · FindingsKey findings and limitations

Key findings

  1. Bayesian evidence decides when fog can be trusted: low sensor sensitivity flips the plan to MOF.
  2. The CSP blocks unsafe or under-powered plans and explains which rule failed.
  3. The agent refuses instead of guessing when inputs are missing or invalid.

Limitations and next steps

  • The prior, sensor values, energy rates and fog settings are assumptions, not field measurements.
  • The MOF model has no humidity-dependent isotherm, so its yield is flat in RH.
  • Fog (2 m²) and MOF (1 kg) are not matched in size, so the ranking does not prove one technology is better.
  • Next: local fog, wind and RH data; measured energy and water; water-quality tests; live sensors with operator approval.

The full prototype runs as a Streamlit app (python -m streamlit run app.py). The operator enters weather, sensor, energy and device settings, reviews the recommended plan with its reasons, and approves or rejects it. Every run can be exported as CSV.
